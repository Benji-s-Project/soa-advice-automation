7 Risks & Limitations

  • All recommended covers are subject to underwriting by Partners Life. Premiums may vary, and exclusions may be applied based on medical or lifestyle disclosures.
  • Stand-down periods apply to trauma cover — typically 90 days from policy commencement for cancer-related conditions.
  • Income protection has a 4-week (James) and 8-week (Sarah) waiting period before benefits commence.
  • Level premiums are guaranteed not to increase due to age but may be adjusted by the insurer due to portfolio-wide changes.
  • Health insurance through Southern Cross has been reviewed and is adequate — no changes recommended at this time.
